TATTLE
A menu bar app that shows what is running on your Mac out of sight.

TO INSTALL
1. Drag Tattle onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Open Tattle from your Applications folder.

macOS WILL WARN YOU THE FIRST TIME
Tattle is not signed with a paid Apple developer account, so macOS will
say it could not verify the app. To allow it:
1. Try to open Tattle once and dismiss the warning.
2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
   "Open Anyway" next to the message about Tattle.
3. Confirm with your password or Touch ID.
Only do this if you trust the person who gave you this app.

THEN
Click the eye icon in your menu bar. A blue card explains two optional
permissions (Full Disk Access and Accessibility) that let Tattle see more.
Tattle never connects to the internet; everything stays on your Mac.

TO REMOVE
Quit Tattle from its gear menu, drag it to the Trash, and delete the
folder  ~/Library/Application Support/Tattle
